## Core Works & Research / 代表作品与研究书稿

1. **Feature Film / 实验电影长片 —《人类投降派》(*Shoot Self with You*, 2023–2024)**
   - Initiated, directed, produced, and edited by He Fa; collectively co-directed and co-written with the P4 Theater ensemble.
   - Official Film Portal: [https://p4theater.cn/shoot-self-with-you/](https://p4theater.cn/shoot-self-with-you/) · [Work Record](https://hefa.p4theater.cn/work/human-surrender) · [IMDb tt43648907](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt43648907/)

   - Full Text & 8 Plates: [https://hefa.p4theater.cn/reading/he-fa-critical-monograph](https://hefa.p4theater.cn/reading/he-fa-critical-monograph) · DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23634125](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23634125) · [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/hehefa/he-fa-critical-monograph)

   - Full Illustrated Text (64 images & 23 references): [https://hefa.p4theater.cn/reading/he-fa-art-practice](https://hefa.p4theater.cn/reading/he-fa-art-practice)
